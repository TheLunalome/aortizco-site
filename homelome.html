04 · storage

Capacity is only useful when recovery has a story too.

Homelome keeps capacity, virtual-machine disks, and backup storage as distinct concerns. A disk being online is a useful signal, but it is not a restore drill.

Four online storage backends

ZFSprimary capacity pool
PBSbackup target role
Localhost and VM volumes

Current control-plane data shows ZFS, Proxmox Backup Server, and local volumes online. The operating rule is more demanding: backup coverage needs evidence, and every meaningful recovery plan needs a test.

07 · field notes

The most important upgrades are usually unglamorous.

The lab is built around the things failures taught me: say what you know, name what you do not, and keep the recovery path shorter than the incident report.

Monitoring beats vibes. A service that looks fine on a dashboard can still be broken.

Operating principle
Why publish a public-safe architecture page?

Architecture is more useful when it explains decisions. This guide can show the boundaries, roles, and tradeoffs without making the home environment easier to target.

What counts as verified here?

A statement earns that label only when a current read-only check supports it. Historical notes are still valuable, but they are marked as context until a fresh check agrees.

What happens after an outage?

The goal is a small evidence trail: symptom, actual cause, alert behavior, repair, and the guardrail added afterward. That is more useful than a perfect-looking dashboard.
